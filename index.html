A little recap

We met at Williams College in the early spring of 2020, Kyle a senior and Elizabeth a sophomore. Like the start of every great love story, we were promptly swept off campus by the pandemic but decided to stay together.

From then, we spent some years shuttling between Williamstown and Manhattan, and others between Southie and Beacon Hill in Boston. In September of 2024, we packed up the car and drove out to San Francisco together.

Throughout the years, Kyle (an only child) has adopted 4 new siblings, Elizabeth has learned to sometimes give him quiet time, and Kyle has earned his Welch stripes on the ski mountain, even when he sneaks off to apres early.

We absolutely love San Francisco and often find ourselves going up north, across the bridge. As a place we visited in the early days with Kyle’s wonderful parents, Healdsburg holds a special place in our heart.

Thank you so much to each of you for supporting us in ways big and small over the years. We cannot wait to celebrate all together in May!
